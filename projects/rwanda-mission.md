# Rwanda Mission and Calendar

Establish Rwanda as the first STUDX operational hub for partnerships, skills, pilots, trade enablement, and locally governed infrastructure.

Workstreams: local ownership; regulatory/data-protection readiness; coffee, agent, robotics/drone, and digital-service pilots; infrastructure and talent; evidence-based review.

| Cadence | Session | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | Mission stand-up | blockers and owners |
| Monday | Programme planning | outcomes and risks |
| Wednesday | Partner/pilot review | decisions and follow-ups |
| Friday | Safety/impact review | evidence and lessons |
| Monthly | Rwanda steering | go/hold/stop decisions |
| Quarterly | Africa readiness gate | expansion recommendation |

Attendee details and meeting links belong in the approved calendar, not Git.
